## 🚀 Open Source at Hoslift

We build open-source software for developers — focused on practical ideas, thoughtful engineering, and great developer experiences.

### 🌱 What We're Building

Our open-source work explores:

- 🤖 AI-powered software
- 🛠️ Developer tools
- ⚡ Frameworks and libraries
- ☁️ Infrastructure and cloud tooling
- 🧪 Experimental developer technologies

Our first major open-source project is **[Ranu.js](https://github.com/hoslift/ranu.js)**, an independent, TypeScript-first full-stack web framework currently in Public Alpha.

### 🤖 Building for AI Agents and Human Developers

We are developing Ranu.js with a forward-looking vision: to create a full-stack framework that makes software development easier for both AI agents and human developers.

Our goal is to bring AI agents and human developers together through a unified development experience, where AI agents can understand, navigate, and work with projects alongside human developers.

We believe the future of software development lies in seamless collaboration between AI agents and human developers. Ranu.js is our vision for making that collaboration a natural part of building modern software.

### 🔭 Looking Ahead

We're at the beginning of our open-source journey.

Our goal is to keep experimenting, building, and releasing useful technologies in the open while growing a community around the work.

We welcome developers who want to explore the projects, contribute code, test ideas, improve documentation, report issues, or simply follow along.
